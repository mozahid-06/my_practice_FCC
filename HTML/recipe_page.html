How to make a PIZZA!

Pizza is a timeless favorite that brings together bold flavors, simple preparation, and endless customization. While traditional pizzas require time-consuming dough-rising, this quick mini pizza recipe uses flatbread or pita to deliver a delicious, hot, personal pie in under twenty minutes. It is the ultimate hassle-free meal for a quick lunch, a late-night craving, or a casual weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

  • 1 round flatbread, pita, or pre-made mini pizza base
  • 2–3 tbsp pizza sauce (or marinara)
  • ½ cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Optional toppings: Pepperoni, sliced mushrooms, diced bell peppers, or fresh basil

Instructions

Follow these steps in order to assemble and bake your pizza:

  1. Preheat the oven: Set your oven to 400°F (200°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Prep the base: Lay the flatbread or pita on the baking sheet and brush the bare edges lightly with olive oil to help them brown and get crispy.
  3. Add the sauce: Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the bread, leaving a small border around the outer edge.
  4. Layer the cheese and toppings: Sprinkle the mozzarella cheese evenly across the sauce, then scatter your choice of toppings over the top.
  5. Bake: Place in the oven for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and slightly golden.
  6. Slice and serve: Remove from the oven, let it cool for a minute, slice into pieces, and enjoy warm.
